myDealz Filter

Hide postings by keywords

Enter keywords separated by commas. Postings with these terms in the title will be hidden.

How to use:

  1. Enter keywords you want to filter separated by commas
  2. Click "Save Filters"
  3. Visit mydealz.de - postings with those keywords will be hidden
  4. The icon shows how many postings are hidden
