Click in this field to unlock editing. Enter keywords separated by commas. Postings containing these terms will NOT be hidden, even if they also contain filter terms. Example: If you filter 'Apple' but add 'Android' as an exception, a post titled 'Apple and Android phone deals' won't be hidden.

How to use:

  1. Enter keywords you want to filter separated by commas
  2. Click "Save Filters"
  3. Visit mydealz.de - postings with those keywords will be hidden
  4. The icon shows how many postings are hidden
